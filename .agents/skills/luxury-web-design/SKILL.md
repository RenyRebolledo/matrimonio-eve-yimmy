# Luxury Web Design & Editorial Aesthetic Master Guide

## 1. Design Philosophy: "Luxury Editorial"
1. **Never look generic or bootstrap-like**: Avoid harsh default colors, standard boxed layouts, and robotic form inputs.
2. **Harmonious Color Systems (60-30-10 Rule)**:
   - **Base (60%)**: Warm alabaster, silk cream (`#FAF7F2`), soft pearl, or muted blush linen.
   - **Structural/Contrast (30%)**: Deep rosewood, terracotta, sage eucalyptus (`#4A6B53`), or deep navy bronze.
   - **Accent (10%)**: Warm gold foil metallic gradients, champagne shimmer, delicate burgundy.
3. **Typography Mastery**:
   - **Editorial Headlines**: Cormorant Garamond, Italiana, Playfair Display, Cinzel (high-contrast serifs).
   - **Script / Calligraphy Accents**: Pinyon Script, Alex Brush, Great Vibes (used sparingly for names & dates).
   - **UI & Reading**: Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Outfit (clean, ultra-legible, balanced line-heights).

## 2. Interactive Experience & Micro-Interactions
- **Glassmorphism & Frosted Glass**: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` with subtle border-highlights `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
- **Envelope / Wax Seal Opening Experience**: Interactive opening sequence on first load.
- **Floating Particles**: Subtle romantic floral petal drift or gentle golden sparkles using HTML5 Canvas or CSS keyframes.
- **Smooth Scroll & Parallax**: Staggered scroll reveals with IntersectionObserver or CSS scroll-driven animations.
- **Mobile First UX**: 100% thumb-friendly buttons, bottom-sheet style modals, responsive cards that fit cleanly on any smartphone screen.

## 3. Component Standards
- **Countdown**: Elegant circular or minimalist badge counters.
- **RSVP**: Seamless multi-step or bottom-sheet modal with immediate feedback and instant WhatsApp generation.
- **Audio Experience**: Minimalist vinyl/soundwave floating pill.
- **Photo Gallery**: Masonry or polaroid layout with smooth lightbox preview.
